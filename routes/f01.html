Frequently Asked Questions — Bus F01

Essential transit details before taking Route F01

Bus F01 operates between 05:30 AM - 00:30 AM. On regular weekdays (Monday through Thursday), the earliest departure leaves at 05:30 and the final trip departs at 00:30 with coaches departing every 8 minutes during peak periods.

The standard one-way fare for Bus F01 is AED 3.00. Passengers can pay seamlessly using their RTA Nol Card (Silver, Gold, or Personal). Ensure your card has at least AED 7.50 minimum required balance before tapping in.
