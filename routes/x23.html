Frequently Asked Questions — Bus X23

Essential transit details before taking Route X23

Bus X23 operates between 05:30 AM - 00:00 Midnight. On regular weekdays (Monday through Thursday), the earliest departure leaves at 05:30 and the final trip departs at 00:00 with coaches departing every 10 minutes during peak periods.

The standard one-way fare for Bus X23 is AED 5.00. Passengers can pay seamlessly using their RTA Nol Card (Silver, Gold, or Personal). Ensure your card has at least AED 7.50 minimum required balance before tapping in.
