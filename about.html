About UAE Bus Time

Empowering commuters, residents, and visitors with frictionless transit schedules across the UAE.

Our Mission

UAE Bus Time was created with a straightforward mission: to eliminate the friction of navigating public bus systems across the United Arab Emirates. Whether you are commuting to work in Business Bay, traveling between Dubai and Sharjah on an intercity express, or visiting the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi, finding the exact bus schedule, route number, and nearest bus stop should be instantaneous.

Unlike heavy transit apps burdened by mandatory registrations, intrusive personal tracking, or complex multi-tiered menus, UAE Bus Time provides direct, high-speed access to bus schedules and stop insights in both English and Arabic.

Comprehensive Transit Coverage

We aggregate and structure publicly available schedule data and official timetables across all major regional transport networks:

  • Dubai RTA: Regular city routes, Express buses, and Metro Feeder lines (F-routes).
  • Abu Dhabi ITC / Darb: Island, mainland, and regional bus connections.
  • Sharjah Roads & Transport Authority (SRTA): City and inter-emirate transit routes.
  • Intercity Express Lines: Direct connections between Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, and Umm Al Quwain.

Spatial Exploration Collaboration: UAE Bus Time works in inspiration and collaboration with UAE Days (uaedays.vercel.app) — an innovative spatial directory mapping key landmarks, transit hubs, and cultural destinations across the Emirates.

Key Values & Principles

1. Zero Sign-up & Total Privacy

You should never have to surrender personal data or create an account just to know when the next bus is arriving. UAE Bus Time operates 100% registration-free.

2. Offline Resilience

Commuters often experience spotty connectivity in underground tunnels or cross-emirate deserts. Our app enables direct downloads of official PDF timetables and local caching for favorite routes.

3. Bilingual First

With a diverse population and millions of annual tourists, full English and Arabic language support is baked into every route screen and stop directory.
