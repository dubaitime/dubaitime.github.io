Public Transport Informational Notice • Year 2026

1. Government & Authority Non-Affiliation Notice

Important Notice: UAE Bus Time is an independent software application created and maintained by Karinov. This application is NOT officially associated, endorsed, sponsored, or affiliated with the Roads and Transport Authority of Dubai (RTA), Integrated Transport Centre Abu Dhabi (ITC / Darb), Sharjah Roads and Transport Authority (SRTA), or any government ministry or official department in the United Arab Emirates.

2. Informational Purpose Only

The schedules, route maps, departure times, trip counts, PDF timetable downloads, and bus stop details provided within this application and website are aggregated from public transport publications and open information channels solely for general informational and navigational convenience.

3. Real-Time Variations & Schedule Changes

Public transportation operations are inherently dynamic and can change without prior warning due to various operational factors, including:

  • Road accidents, unexpected traffic congestion, or construction diversions.
  • Special events, public holidays, Eid holiday timetables, and Ramadan operational hours.
  • Weather incidents such as rainstorms, fog alerts, or sandstorms.
  • Official route restructuring, temporary station closures, or fleet adjustments by transport authorities.

Consequently, all timings provided in the app should be treated as estimations. Travelers are strongly encouraged to verify urgent travel requirements through official municipal channels (such as the RTA Smart App or Darb Abu Dhabi).

4. Nol Card, Fares, & Ticketing Disclaimer

UAE Bus Time does not issue transit tickets, process payments, recharge Nol cards, or calculate dynamic promotional fares. All transit fare payments must be completed via official channels (such as physical Nol cards, Hafilat cards, or approved government payment terminals).
