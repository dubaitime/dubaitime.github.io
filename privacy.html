Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Application Package: com.karinov.uae.bustiming

1. Introduction & Overview

At UAE Bus Time (developed by Karinov), we are staunch believers in consumer digital privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application and landing website handle information when you use our public transit timing services.

Core Principle: UAE Bus Time is designed from the ground up to operate without requiring any user registration, personal account creation, or transmission of personal identification data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Social media accounts or credentials.
  • Financial, billing, or Nol card payment details.
  • Personal transit history or individualized movement profiles.

3. Location Data (GPS Permissions)

The mobile application contains an optional "Nearby Stops" feature designed to calculate distance to the nearest transit hubs:

  • On-Device Calculation: Location data (latitude and longitude) is accessed strictly on your local device when you explicitly tap "Use My GPS".
  • No Tracking: Your GPS location is never transmitted to our remote servers, never stored in any database, and never sold or shared with third parties or advertising networks.
  • Optional Use: You can completely decline location permissions and still search all bus schedules, stops, and PDF timetables manually by station name or landmark.

4. Local Storage on Device

To provide a convenient offline commuter experience, the app stores certain preferences locally on your physical device (using local storage / SQLite / shared preferences):

  • Bookmarked / favorite bus routes (e.g. Route 30, E303).
  • Selected UI language (English or Arabic).
  • Downloaded PDF schedule files for offline viewing.

This data resides solely on your handset and is deleted if you clear app cache or uninstall the application.

5. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website and application may provide external links to verified platforms, including Google Play Store for downloads, and partner spatial exploration directories such as UAE Days. We encourage you to review the individual privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13 in a manner that collects personal information. Because no personal data is collected from any user, our platform is completely safe for general family use.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or legal compliance standards. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated modification date.
